Brightside is a place for questions, half-finished ideas, happy accidents, and the confidence to try again.

Room for every kind of thinker.

Some students arrive with a game idea. Some arrive wondering what a button does. Both are exactly where they need to be.

Our mentors make space for experimenting, sharing, and finding your own way through a challenge.

  • Small groupsEveryone gets time to ask, build, and show what they made.
  • Real projectsWe learn skills by making things you can play with and share.
  • Kind collaborationWe celebrate questions and help each other get unstuck.
