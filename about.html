A little more about us

Code is for everyone.

Brightside Code Club is a creative coding school where kids aged 9–13 learn to build websites, games, and bold new ideas in a kind, encouraging space.

Our Mission

We help young people feel confident making things with technology, not just consuming it.

Our mentors turn curiosity into practical skills through playful projects, patient guidance, and plenty of room for each student’s own point of view.

  • Make with confidenceStart small, try often, and learn that mistakes are part of making.
  • Find your voiceUse code as a creative language for ideas that are uniquely yours.
  • Build togetherShare discoveries, ask good questions, and make space for other curious minds.
